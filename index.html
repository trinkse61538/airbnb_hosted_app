How it works

From property details to one simple guest link.

You provide the information. We organize it into a polished guest experience that can be shared before every stay.

Send property details

Photos, address, arrival steps, parking, Wi-Fi, amenities and checkout notes.

We build your guide

The content is organized into a clean mobile-first layout tailored to your property.

Review & refine

You check the instructions and we refine the presentation before it goes live.

Share one link
